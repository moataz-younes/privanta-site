**Headline:** Compliance Theater Is Expensive Failure

Your current approach treats compliance as periodic projects and policy documents — creating dangerous gaps between what's written and what's enforced. This misalignment between legal requirements and IT execution leaves you exposed to regulatory penalties and operational failures.

- **Audit paralysis:** 90-day preparation cycles that halt business operations  
- **Control gaps:** Policies exist but lack real-time enforcement mechanisms
